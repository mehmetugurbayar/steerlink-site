FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need a wheel or extra hardware?

No. Your phone and your PC on the same Wi‑Fi are all it takes.

How fast is it?

Inputs go straight over your local network. Your phone shows the live latency the whole time, so you always know.

Is the PC app free?

Yes, always. Pro is bought once on your phone and works with any PC.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-up. Purchases stay with your App Store or Google Play account.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, as keyboard input. The virtual Xbox controller is Windows only for now.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click "More info", then "Run anyway". New apps show this until they build up reputation.
